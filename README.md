### 🎥 Multimedia Portfolio
This portfolio is a collection of projects focused on digital education, software prototypes, and social entrepreneurship.

>  **Note on Video Quality:** Embedded video players use compressed previews for smooth playback. Original HD versions are available in [`videos/originals/`](./videos/originals/).

#### 📁 Featured Projects

**1. Dyslexia Application (Prototype)**
A mobile application designed to support individuals with dyslexia, developed based on a user needs survey.
*   **Technologies:** Angular, Ionic, Java (Android Studio), DigitalOcean, and SQL.
*   **Features:** Educational modules, reading and writing exercises, and communication with special education professionals.

**2. Sun Energy & Green Initiative**
This is my **first comprehensive project**, created right after completing my secondary education to prove that ambition and a solid idea do not depend on age. The project raises awareness about PM10 particles and offers a concrete entrepreneurial solution.
*   **Ecological Analysis:** Research on the health impacts of pollution and PM10 particles according to the WHO.
*   **Entrepreneurship:** Crowdfunding campaign on **Indiegogo** to build a photovoltaic power plant.
